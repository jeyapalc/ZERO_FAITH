# Manifesto language and historical reference audit

Public research and case records checked on 17 November 2026. This audit compares the three ZERO masterlists and the local NVE pilot against selected published analyses of attacker-authored or attributed material. It covers documented examples from older attacks and recent research, including incidents in 2024–2026. It does not inventory every manifesto or claim that all authorship questions are settled.

## Gaps closed in the local pilot

- **Conspiracy narratives.** `Great Replacement`, `white genocide`, and Canadian French `grand remplacement` were absent. They are now enabled for document retrieval. The Christchurch inquiry and Bellaiche supply the English terminology. The Government of Canada guide supplies the French wording. `Eurabia`, `cultural Marxism`, `race war` and `blood libel` are broader contextual searches and remain optional. [Christchurch inquiry](https://christchurchattack.royalcommission.nz/the-report/part-2-context/harmful-behaviours-right-wing-extremism-and-radicalisation) · [Canadian French guide](https://www.canada.ca/fr/patrimoine-canadien/campagnes/lutte-contre-islamophobie-canada/ressources/guide-combattre-islamophobie.html) · [Tree of Life study](https://doi.org/10.1002/bsl.70066)
- **Events can stand in for names.** The Poway plea announcement says John Earnest cited the attacks in Pittsburgh and Christchurch. The search now includes `Tree of Life synagogue shooting`, `Poway synagogue shooting`, two official Christchurch event names, and `Halle synagogue attack`. `John Earnest`, `Stephan Balliet` and the full name `Robert Gregory Bowers` remain optional person references. A citation to an incident can be ordinary reporting, remembrance or claimed inspiration. [Poway plea announcement](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/john-earnest-pleads-guilty-113-count-federal-hate-crime-indictment-connection-poway) · [Christchurch inquiry](https://christchurchattack.royalcommission.nz/the-report/executive-summary-2/executive-summary) · [Halle forensic study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/elements/abs/decoding-terrorism/D6AC34F2DD0DC4F507A1E0545898E044)
- **Less familiar historical references.** The optional pass adds Juraj Krajčík and the Bratislava case, where forensic authors describe a Terrorgram connection. Their possible co-authorship finding is an inference. The optional pass also adds Pekka Eric Auvinen, Jokela High School, Vladislav Roslyakov and Arda Küçükyetim. The latter three person references in the Madison material are reported by the TCC paper based on material attributed to the attacker. Powell’s analysis of Auvinen’s own writing is separate evidence. [ARC Bratislava study](https://www.accresearch.org/accreports/terrorgrams-first-saint) · [CTC TCC study](https://ctc.westpoint.edu/true-crime-community-understanding-the-depths-of-digital-fandom-and-performative-violence/) · [Powell corpus](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acorp.2025.100160)
- **Orthography and list coverage.** ZERO_FAITH already has `blackpill`, `blackpilled` and `going ER`. The local pilot adds the sourced literal variants `black pill` and `black-pill` as optional rows. ZERO_NAZ already contains published numeric and slogan references, including `14 Words` and `1488`. Keep generic numbers and acronyms out of the default scan. ## What still needs a reviewer

Use each matched phrase to navigate to the source artifact. Read enough context to distinguish the speaker’s own words from a quotation or forwarded item. Log whether the passage names, describes, endorses or claims influence from a person or event. A repeated phrase can come from a common source, and a claimed influence can be self-presentation. Use independent records when deciding how strong the relationship is. Humor, images, initials and allusions can still produce misses. Keep visual review separate and track which source formats were searched.

This pass added 23 cited strings to the local pilot. Eight are enabled. The other 15 are available for a focused pass. For the next coverage check, annotate unseen material before running Find. Include a reporting or counterspeech example for each major narrative and compare new relevant passages with new irrelevant hits. The local pilot does not contain raw attacker writings.
