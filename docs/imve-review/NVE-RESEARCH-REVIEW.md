# Nihilistic violent extremism research for keyword review

Public sources checked on 17 November 2026. This review supports literal searches of authorized records in ZERO_NIHL. The companion `NVE-RESEARCH-PILOT.tsv` retains a source and review note for each term.

The [manifesto language review](NVE-MANIFESTO-REFERENCE-AUDIT.md) documents the later cross-check of published forensic studies and case records, including the additions it yielded.

## Plan and assessment

Read the full ISD, GIFCT and GNET paper. Compare its terminology with Canadian government usage and check selected 2024–2026 incidents against public inquiry findings, police releases and court outcomes. Record each documented relationship by type: organization, reference to a prior attacker, observed visual theme, claimed allegiance or evidence of coordination. Compare the resulting terms with the published ZERO_NIHL masterlist.

The paper provides vocabulary and descriptions of the communities that its researchers observed. Its examples and follow-up monitoring cannot establish how widespread those patterns are. Official sources carry more weight for the events or proceedings they describe. A charge release establishes that a charge was laid, and a prosecutor’s account of another incident remains attributed to the prosecutor. Incident selection is illustrative. It includes files with unresolved questions and a Canadian comparison case outside this research classification.

## What the full paper contains

*Beyond Extremism* distinguishes several overlapping communities. The Com is a wider network. ISD situates 764 within it and describes coercion, exploitation and violence glorification in parts of that network. No Lives Matter appears both as a group name and an expression. In the material researchers studied, the True Crime Community (TCC) includes fandom around prior mass killers, with art, video and writing that can celebrate perpetrators. These are different relationship types. Treat each name as a pointer to the relevant passage. [Full paper, sections 1–2](https://gifct.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/GNETGIFCTISD-NVE-Report.pdf)

The paper describes recurring imagery drawn from neo-Nazi, occult and serial-killer references. It observes that images and edits can convey admiration or shock value even when accompanying text says little. Racism, antisemitism and misogyny can also be substantive themes when the text or conduct supports that reading. In the research profile, record **what the content depicts, how it is framed, and whose conduct the original record supports**. Keep those three observations distinct. [Full paper, sections 2 and 4](https://gifct.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/GNETGIFCTISD-NVE-Report.pdf) · [CSIS 2025 public report, Operations and analysis](https://www.canada.ca/en/security-intelligence-service/corporate/publications/csis-public-report-2025/operations-and-analysis.html)

The rest of the paper maps sexual exploitation, cyber harms and real-world violence to platform policies, then discusses prevention and support. Its November 2026 follow-up uses a limited sample of posts found by researchers and explicitly describes platform access constraints. The authors’ reported counts combine completed and disrupted incidents under their own linkage criteria. They are useful for locating cases to examine and cannot give a keyword eligibility rule or a measured hit rate for ZERO_NIHL. [Full paper, sections 2–4](https://gifct.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/GNETGIFCTISD-NVE-Report.pdf) · [ISD November 2026 follow-up](https://www.isdglobal.org/digital-dispatch/tiktok-and-the-mainstreaming-of-the-true-crime-community/)

## Canadian terminology and anchors

CSIS places NVE within its IMVE framework and explains that much activity in this space falls below its national security threshold. ISD uses a research definition centred on misanthropy and violence without a coherent ideological objective. Retain both attributions. Canada's register lists 764, Maniac Murder Cult and Terrorgram Collective separately. The register establishes Canadian listing status. West Shore RCMP describes 764 as part of the broader Com network. Preserve that relationship in the dictionary rather than treating the two names as interchangeable. [CSIS 2025 public report](https://www.canada.ca/en/security-intelligence-service/corporate/publications/csis-public-report-2025/operations-and-analysis.html) · [Public Safety Canada register](https://www.publicsafety.gc.ca/cnt/ntnl-scrt/cntr-trrrsm/lstd-ntts/crrnt-lstd-ntts-en.aspx) · [West Shore RCMP, 9 June 2026](https://rcmp.ca/en/bc/west-shore/news/2026/06/4353942)

The French version of the CSIS report uses `extrémisme violent à caractère nihiliste` and `EVCN`. These are sourced Canadian terms for searching documents written in French. The short form stays off in the pilot. [SCRS, Opérations et analyse](https://www.canada.ca/fr/service-renseignement-securite/organisation/publications/rapport-public-du-scrs-2025/operations-et-analyse.html)

A 2017 DOJ release confirms the spelling `Dylann Roof` and his federal hate-crime convictions. A later community’s reference to an offender does not transfer the original offender’s motive. Keep names and incident names in an optional historical-reference pass. ## U.S. anti-government historical references

Ruby Ridge and the 1993 Waco siege recur as grievance references in U.S. militia narratives. ISD links Waco to Timothy McVeigh’s motivation for the 1995 Oklahoma City bombing. In separate research, Bellaiche documents an example of later Saints Culture imagery that represented McVeigh alongside other perpetrators. These sources support looking for the event and the name in context, with later veneration logged separately from the originating event. [DOJ Inspector General chronology](https://oig.justice.gov/archives/reports/EOUSA/e0401/appd.htm) · [ISD militia research](https://www.isdglobal.org/isd-explainer/militias-in-the-us/) · [ICSR study](https://icsr.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ICSR-Report-Imagined-Threats-Demographic-Conspiracy-Theories-Antisemitism-and-the-Legacy-of-the-2018-Pittsburgh-Synagogue-Attack.pdf)

The FBI lists Marvin Heemeyer and Killdozer as militia-related historical references. ADL researchers observed some Boogaloo pages glorifying Heemeyer. This documents later adoption of the reference. A news story, local history reference or repost can contain the same words. [FBI symbols guide](https://vault.fbi.gov/domestic-terrorism-symbols-guide/Domestic%20Terrorism%20Symbols%20Guide%20Part%2001%20%28Final%29) · [ADL monitoring](https://www.adl.org/resources/article/one-year-after-facebook-takes-steps-remove-boogaloo-content-movement-resurfaces)

The pilot enables `Ruby Ridge`, `Waco siege`, `Waco standoff`, `Killdozer`, `Marvin Heemeyer`, `Oklahoma City bombing` and `Timothy McVeigh` for ordinary literal Find. `Waco` by itself stays off because it is also a city name. When a focused search returns both `Waco` and `Waco siege` on the same line, count one event reference in the passage.

Checking the same source family identified the 2014 Bundy ranch standoff and the 2016 Malheur refuge occupation. Both event phrases are enabled. The shorter refuge name stays opt-in because it also occurs outside discussion of the occupation. The ICSR study also supports an optional entry for Robert Bowers as a documented figure of later veneration. For future additions, audit U.S. anti-government events alongside NVE and TCC research, Canadian case records, and work on visual culture. For each source, check for gaps in named incidents, individuals and alternate spellings. Test the ordinary enabled-only search as well as the complete list. An entry that is off does not close a gap in the default search.

## Search recommendations and next step

The published ZERO_NIHL masterlist already contains `764`, `Com Network`, `No Lives Matter` and several Maniac Murder Cult aliases. The pilot targets gaps: `The Com`, `True Crime Community`, the official French CSIS term, titles describing offender glorification, and full names for a separate historical-reference pass. `The Com` is mapped to the umbrella network. `TCC`, `EVCN`, full names of perpetrators, and occult or extremist group names used as cross-references start disabled. Retain `764` as contextual because numeric identifiers routinely collide. [Published ZERO_NIHL masterlist](https://github.com/jeyapalc/ZERO_NIHL/blob/main/lists/masterlist.tsv)

Compare the pilot with the masterlist on authorized, de-identified excerpts. Run `Find-WordList.ps1` with the pilot TSV and `-WholeWord`. In Excel, import the TSV as UTF-8 text and follow the `Terms` table instructions. Record new relevant passages, irrelevant hits, and missed references. Include news coverage, counterspeech, unrelated acronyms and victim disclosures. Run a separate pass for optional names and acronyms to measure their added workload. Keep the source, language and note for any terms promoted to the masterlist. [PowerShell manual](https://github.com/jeyapalc/ZERO_NIHL/blob/main/manual/POWERSHELL.md) · [Excel manual](https://github.com/jeyapalc/ZERO_NIHL/blob/main/manual/EXCEL.md)

This review used public records, research and agency summaries. It did not access confidential files, raw device extractions or a representative set of pre-incident documents. Results from this pilot can establish retrieval coverage for a chosen corpus.
