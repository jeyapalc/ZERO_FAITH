# Where the ZERO_NIHL keyword pilot is likely to miss relevant records

Review of work published from late 2023 through 2026. The local pilot contained 38 terms when reviewed, eight enabled by default. I compared it with the published ZERO_NIHL masterlist and used the paper dates separately from the periods covered by their evidence.

## Evidence and its limits

| Publication | What the authors examined | What this can tell us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Julien Bellaiche, *Imagined Threats*, 2023 | Court records and historical discourse, plus online observation in July and August 2023 around the 2018 Pittsburgh synagogue attack. | Documents the later veneration of Robert Bowers and visual references to prior attackers in particular far-right spaces. Scope is that case and those spaces. [ICSR and George Washington University report](https://icsr.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ICSR-Report-Imagined-Threats-Demographic-Conspiracy-Theories-Antisemitism-and-the-Legacy-of-the-2018-Pittsburgh-Synagogue-Attack.pdf) |
| *Media Coverage of School Shootings*, November 2023 | Distortion analysis using publicly known U.S. K–12 shootings with injury between 1990 and 2016. | Finds that media coverage varies with incident and perpetrator characteristics. Explains why a list drawn from prominent stories may miss lesser-covered cases. This is a historical U.S. sample, not an estimate for today’s NVE material. [NIJ research abstract and publication link](https://nij.ojp.gov/library/publications/media-coverage-school-shootings-distortion-analysis-incident-and-perpetrator) |

## Blind spots and fixes

1. **Historical references outside the usual names.** The pilot includes Tarrant, Roof and Columbine-related terms. It omits Robert Bowers, whom Bellaiche documents as a figure venerated in some far-right spaces. Smith and colleagues report references to Pekka Eric Auvinen and Vladislav Roslyakov in material attributed to the Madison attacker. The authors acknowledge how that material reached them. Treat these as candidate terms for a case reference pass, with the strength and limits of attribution written into each entry. Look for event and place names, full names and source-attested spelling variants as well. Keep the original incident separate from the later reference. [Bellaiche 2023](https://icsr.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ICSR-Report-Imagined-Threats-Demographic-Conspiracy-Theories-Antisemitism-and-the-Legacy-of-the-2018-Pittsburgh-Synagogue-Attack.pdf) · [Smith et al. 2026](https://ctc.westpoint.edu/true-crime-community-understanding-the-depths-of-digital-fandom-and-performative-violence/)
2. **Image, audio and adjacent conversation.** A tribute can rely on the choice of image or on editing, and meaning can depend on the surrounding exchange. The scanner searches exported text line by line. Track which formats were available to search and send relevant media to a separate human review. Where available, a caption, transcript or OCR result can help locate the original. Preserve the artifact ID and time or page location. Review the original item with nearby context. [Allwinn et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1037/tam0000240) · [Smith et al. 2026](https://ctc.westpoint.edu/true-crime-community-understanding-the-depths-of-digital-fandom-and-performative-violence/)
3. **Who is doing what.** The existing masterlist already includes terms for coercion, sextortion and self-harm. The gap is interpretation. The same phrase can appear in a victim disclosure, a forwarded screenshot, an analyst’s note or the original coercive message. Reviewers need the original sender and recipient, what the record shows them doing, and space to record uncertainty. Use separate review labels for reporting or quoting, endorsement, claimed influence and corroborated conduct. [Argentino and Lindsay 2026](https://ctc.westpoint.edu/remotely-coerced-violence-764-the-com-network-and-the-hybridization-of-threats/) · [Archives of Sexual Behavior study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10508-025-03161-y)
4. **Geography, language and time.** The pilot has two French rows, both for the official Canadian classification term. It has little French case vocabulary. The most detailed case study dataset used here is mostly American despite its international sample, and public long-form case files are unevenly available. Build a Canadian French and non-U.S. sample first. Add terms when a regional source shows actual use. Track when the wording was observed as well as when the paper appeared. [Smith et al. 2026](https://ctc.westpoint.edu/true-crime-community-understanding-the-depths-of-digital-fandom-and-performative-violence/) · [French CSIS public report](https://www.canada.ca/fr/service-renseignement-securite/organisation/publications/rapport-public-du-scrs-2025/operations-et-analyse.html)
5. **The evaluation set can hide the shortfall.** Research reports, media coverage and prosecutions tend to make some cases more visible than others. A set built around known group names will mainly tell us that known group names are searchable. Separate the records used to propose terms from those used to evaluate them. Include comparable benign material and records that a human reviewer finds relevant even though they contain no established label. Dedupe repeated copies of the same passage before counting. [NIJ media coverage study](https://nij.ojp.gov/library/publications/media-coverage-school-shootings-distortion-analysis-incident-and-perpetrator) · [Smith et al. 2026, note b](https://ctc.westpoint.edu/true-crime-community-understanding-the-depths-of-digital-fandom-and-performative-violence/)

## Recommended next pass

Keep the current pilot as a baseline. Have a reviewer mark references in a fresh collection before using Find. Compare the existing list with a small batch of source-attested aliases and incident names, then with the historical names enabled. Track newly surfaced relevant passages, extra irrelevant hits, and reviewer-marked passages missed by each pass. Record image and audio items separately so missing source formats are visible. Review whether the added material justifies the time required to clear the extra hits before changing the default. This literature review identifies plausible gaps and several concrete omissions. It does not provide measured retrieval rates for case material.
