# Human review

Record observed conduct, provenance, dates, context, corroboration, counterevidence, and protective circumstances. Distinguish endorsement from quotation, reporting, education, satire, counterspeech, and victim disclosure. A term match cannot prove identity, membership, or intent.

Canadian policy categories and legal entity designations answer different questions. Research classifications may be stale, contested, or jurisdiction-specific. Fitness activities, protest, religious beliefs, and political advocacy do not establish extremism.

Support potential victims and use the forum's threat-response process. Credible immediate danger in Canada requires prompt attention through 9-1-1. No reports are sent automatically.

The matcher normalizes NFKC, case, format characters, and whitespace. It uses Unicode letter/digit boundaries, with underscores as separators. It does not infer context, resolve negation, inspect images, recognize all variants, preserve original offsets, or track behavior over time. TXT exports have no context intelligence.
